About Me

Hi! I'm Thanh Long (Oliver) Vu, currently pursuing an MPhil in Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence (MLMI) at the University of Cambridge, where I am a member of Downing College.

My research lies at the intersection of computational statistics, probabilistic machine learning and efficient inference, with particular interests in simulation-based inference, multi-fidelity methods, Stein discrepancies, and efficient evaluation of language models. I previously completed a Bachelor of Mathematics (Honours) and a Bachelor of Data Science at Queensland University of Technology (QUT). During my Honours, I worked with Dr David J Warne on multi-fidelity simulation-based inference.

Alongside research, I have professional experience in data science and analytics across consulting, insurance, and applied AI, including Quantium, Suncorp Group, and PwC Australia. I have also spent approximately three years teaching programming, data science, and statistics at QUT.

I'm interested in pursuing opportunities in ML research, applied science, quantitative research, and doctoral study, and welcome conversations about research collaboration.
